## What this adds

The **Cursor harness**: Tightbeam can spawn, gate, and retire sessions running `cursor-agent` (ACP), alongside claude/codex. Built on a new **binary-native (shim) harness provisioning seam** shared with the held OpenCode adapter.

### Highlights

- **Shim seam** (`eb011e2`): provisioning for binary-native adapters launched through a verified shim.
- **Cursor adapter** (`cursor.ex` / `cursor_rails.ex`): rails compiled into Cursor's hooks gate (deny/allow with sentinels), fail-closed catalog, 0-LISTEN enforcement, readiness rendezvous.
