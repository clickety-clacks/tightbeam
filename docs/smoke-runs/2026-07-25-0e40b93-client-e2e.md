  turn that does not call a tool.** Steps 3, 11, 12, 13a fail on it directly;
  5, 8 and 13b fail on it inside the turn each of them nests. Every other
  assertion in those steps held — including 13b's substantive half (set_model
  returned 200, `GET /api/session-status` reported the new base ref, the
  payload satisfied the client's decode contract, and `sessions.model` was
  updated); only the FOLLOWING turn's progress oracle failed.
- Evidence, captured frame-by-frame from the sim client on this leg: a plain
  turn ("hello, who are you?") and an explicitly reasoning-provoking turn
  ("Think step by step: what is 17 * 23?") each produced `ack`, user echo,
  `prompt_turn_state` accepted→running, `typing active=true`, activity, the
  assistant message, `delivered`, `typing active=false` — and ZERO
  `agent_progress` frames. The typing INDICATOR itself is emitted
  unconditionally and correctly; only its LABEL never appears.
- Mechanism: `Tightbeam.Acp.Adapter.progress_status/1` maps exactly two ACP
  updates to a progress line — `agent_thought_chunk` → "Thinking…" and
  `tool_call`/`tool_call_update` → its title. claude-agent-acp sends no
  `agent_thought_chunk` at `claude-sonnet-5[medium]`, so a non-tool turn has
  nothing to map. Step 4 passes because the tool_call title supplies the label.
- **Adjudication needed on SMOKE.md step 3.** Its PASS condition reads "typing
  indicator ON with live progress text (at minimum 'Thinking…' flickers)". That
  parenthetical is falsified on the claude leg and is supported by no matrix
  row: CAP-012 cites SMOKE **step 4** as its proof, and step 4 passes. Two
  readings, and the driver takes neither on its own authority:
  (a) the runbook overstates — the invariant is the indicator, the label is
      best-effort and harness-reported, so step 3's clause should be amended to
      match CAP-012's actual scope; or
  (b) it is a real client-visible degradation — the indicator label stays empty
      for the whole turn on ordinary conversation, which is what the runbook was
      written to catch.
  The oracle was NOT weakened to make the run green; SMOKE is normative and a
  runner-local waiver is exactly what this spec forbids.
- J4 (queueing) and J5 (concurrency) PASS, which is the useful control: the
  substrate's ordering, lane isolation and delivery guarantees all hold. The
  failures are one assertion, not a broken gateway.
