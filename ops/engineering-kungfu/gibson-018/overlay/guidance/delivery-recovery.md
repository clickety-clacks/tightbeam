# Delivery recovery

Identify the unresolved outcome and responsible actor. Distinguish useful ongoing
work, a valid dependency wait, a false assumption, unavailable execution and an
enforced refusal. Missing progress prose alone does not establish a stall.

Correct false assumptions with evidence. Resolve internal sequencing and ownership
within existing authority. Identify the governing restriction behind a refusal and
its responsible decision-maker; resolve through a supported path or escalate the
actual decision required. Keep separable authorized work moving.

For a dependency wait, name the resolver and resolving evidence, and reuse valid
coverage for that obligation. For a bounded checkpoint, preserve the supported
continuation and reassessment point. Choose the form that matches the unfinished
work; a checkpoint does not require an invented external dependency. Route a delivery or suppression failure to its owner.
Use supported correction or recovery when coverage is invalid, without duplicating
healthy subscriptions. After a ruling or recovery changes coverage, verify the surviving
continuation and its current disposition before reporting it. An identifier from the
triggering prompt may now name a canceled wake.

Judge progress against the individual obligation. Activity elsewhere in a shared
session is context, not proof about this assignment. Record material advancement in
its work record; do not manufacture progress attests to reset a supervision clock.
Route a demonstrated false prod or repeated unchanged request to its recovery owner.

When depending on a listener or callback, verify delivery into the accountable
session when establishing it, after changing it, or when delivery is in doubt. A
connected process, an unread log and a queued notification are not proof of receipt.
Silence alone establishes neither health nor failure. Avoid periodic inference turns
whose only purpose is to announce that the listener is still present.

On terminal failure, use the recorded cause and supported repair or parent route.
Name who will arrange renewed execution when the failed session cannot act. Do not
assume the provider or failed holder supplies another continuation.
Reconcile possible external effects before rerunning an unknown outcome. Preserve
the promised result and prior evidence through replacement, and verify that work
actually resumes. A notice, reassignment or delivered wake is not fulfillment.

Keep routine checkpoint evidence in the work record while its recipient is stopped
or blocked. Notify the accountable owner when a material result, changed dependency,
required decision or delivery failure changes what it must do. Preserve agreed
reporting obligations and useful handoffs; do not send repeated receipt-only wakes.
