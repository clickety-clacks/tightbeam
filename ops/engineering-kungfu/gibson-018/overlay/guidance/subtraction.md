# Subtraction

Question mechanisms and process that do not serve the agreed outcome. Consider
removing an unnecessary requirement or accepting a clearly described limitation
when that serves the product better than adding machinery. Changes to intent,
scope or acceptance follow the shared authority boundary.

Use repeated churn as evidence to reconsider the approach. No fixed round count
chooses the remedy. Preserve useful findings in the ordinary work record and make
coherent corrections rather than adding a procedure for each incident.
