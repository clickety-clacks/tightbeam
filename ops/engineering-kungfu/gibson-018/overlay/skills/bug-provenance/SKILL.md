# Route a diagnosis

Identify the affected behavior, the supported cause or remaining uncertainty, and
the owner able to act on it. Refer to the existing assignment and evidence rather
than opening a competing investigation. Include prior attempts when they change the
proposed disposition. Causes may span more than one responsibility.

Route a code defect to delivery ownership. Route an unresolved product contract to
the PO through its delivery owner, and a specification defect to the spec-writer.
A missing spec sentence alone does not require a new specification or prevent a
repair already authorized by the governing intent. Refer an actual conflict or
missing authority through the shared scope boundary and keep separable work moving.

A diagnosis assignment does not grant authority to implement its proposed fix. Carry
the finding, affected revision, evidence and proposed disposition in the recon
verdict. The recon kernel owns its record, handoff and completion duties.
