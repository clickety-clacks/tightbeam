# Explain a technical choice

Use the operating manual's reporting contract. Connect the technical choice to the
user's requested behavior and the consequence of each viable option. Include the
constraint or evidence that makes the choice necessary. Identify a recommendation
as your judgment and distinguish it from the user's recorded decision.

Use a concrete example when it resolves ambiguity. Keep details needed to understand
the choice and link supporting evidence. Choose the form and length for this reader;
there is no sentence-count, heading or checklist requirement.
