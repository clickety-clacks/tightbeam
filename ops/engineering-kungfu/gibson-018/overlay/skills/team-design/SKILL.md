# Team design

Start from the outcome, authority, current artifacts, uncertainty, dependencies,
available capabilities and cost constraints. Reuse adequate analysis. Choose the
smallest process that can deliver the agreed result. A familiar bounded repair may
need only a coder and independent reviewer under its existing orchestrator.

Use an orchestrator as a bounded planner when topology needs strong reasoning but
routine delivery does not. Select the planning activity in preferred-models. Give
the planner the decision to make, relevant evidence, scope and effort boundary.
An Astra or Fable planner can advise a Sol delivery orchestrator. Keep a strong
orchestrator throughout when dependencies require continuous deep judgment. Scale
reasoning to the expected length and complexity, even for a concise deliverable.

As planner, return an actionable recommendation in the ordinary work artifact or
assignment context. Explain only the choices that matter: independent versus coupled
work, owners, specialist outcomes, model activities, critical dependencies, review,
product judgment and what evidence would change the arrangement. Account for the
cost of supervision, repeated context, corrective work and idle roles. Recommend no
new agent when the current team is adequate. Do not require a separate design paper.

As delivery owner, adopt or amend the recommendation within your authority. Create
the actual assignments and addressed relationships using existing dispatch mechanics.
The planner's advice alone does not staff the team or transfer an obligation.

Compose the elected archetypes first: orchestrator, product-owner, spec-writer,
coder, reviewer-code, reviewer-spec and recon. Vary their number, scope, model,
effort and lifetime. Delegate a coupled subproblem to another orchestrator when that
reduces total coordination cost; its owner may redesign that subtree. Avoid another
layer whose only job is forwarding messages. Keep cross-subtree contracts with an
identified delivery owner and arrange direct specialist exchange where useful.

Carry an addressed product role and spirit reference into each relevant brief.
Keep the PO available for proactive product judgment, new specifications and material
intent questions. Give delivery detail to the responsible orchestrator. Escalate
changes that affect another owner's outcome, authority, dependency or decision.

Do not invent an archetype merely to name a task such as planner or integrator.
Use a bounded assignment and relevant skills. If recurring work demonstrates a
missing durable responsibility, propose or author a scoped archetype through the
supported identity workflow within your authority. Load tightbeam-guidance-authoring
for its composition and tightbeam-law-minting for any proposed enforcement. A new
name supplies neither capabilities nor runtime mechanisms by itself.

Reassess when coupling, repeated clarification, corrective supervision, cost or
unowned work contradicts the plan. Change the relevant part and preserve usable work.
Use the shared assignment lifecycle for handoff. Role rebinding and new spawns do
not reparent existing sessions or transfer open assignments. Inspect actual custody
and recovery routes before claiming that a topology repair is complete. Main remains
the substrate's supported last-resort address; do not make it a routine worker.

Keep topology as a judgment made for the job. Enforce concrete authority, provenance,
review and data-integrity boundaries; do not introduce a fixed agent graph, mandatory
planner, stage count or model-provider gate.
