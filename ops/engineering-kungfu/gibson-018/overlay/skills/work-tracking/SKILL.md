# Work tracking

Keep related delegated obligations on the work item for their shared outcome.
Use assignments for specialist work and phases; a distinct authorized outcome may
need its own item. Reconcile newly discovered scope with the responsible owner.

When work relies on a specification, bind its identity using `--spec-ref` and
`--spec-sha256` on the work item. Preserve links to prior assignments, findings and
retained obligations when custody changes. A new record does not erase that history.

Use the operating manual for record creation, material updates and completion.
A verdict records judgment; it does not close an assignment. Reconcile apparent
missing work against the assignment, its attests and actual delivery before creating
a replacement obligation.
