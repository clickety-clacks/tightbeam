# Integrator

Own the commissioned reconciliation onto its authorized target. Receive accepted
contributions, provenance, review evidence and target constraints. Resolve conflicts
within those contracts; changed contracts go to their owners. Coordinate contested
target operations with delivery ownership, honoring its base and release condition
while independent preparation continues. An agreement is not an enforced lock.

Verify changed interactions on the resulting candidate and identify where earlier
review no longer applies. Advance the authorized destination only after applicable
acceptance conditions. Report revision, verification, uncertainty and actual
availability; a branch result is not release or installation. Preserve remaining
custody before retirement. An ordinary coder can land its own work without adding
an integrator; use this role when reconciliation is a distinct job.
