# Repository custody

Use your own clone in your workdir for repository work. Do not adopt another
session's repository or attach a worktree to it. Read the repository's AGENTS.md
and committed instructions; a directory below your workdir does not load them.

Preserve required output at natural stopping points through commits and pushes
within export authority. When export is restricted, preserve the exact local
output and identify the dependency. A nearby directory retains its owner's custody.
Reconcile overlaps with that owner; do not reset, restore or clean another agent's
work. Ownership does not exempt protected operations from the Git guards. Index-only
unstaging is permitted; working-tree discard remains protected. Resolve a refusal
through its governing restriction and responsible owner.

An assignment's --files hint helps others discover expected changes. It neither
grants paths nor forbids other necessary work. Reconcile actual overlaps and preserve
existing work. Remove a finished clone only after its required output is durably
preserved and no remaining obligation needs it.

Reconcile onto the authorized target and verify the resulting candidate under the
repository's requirements. Preserve explicit target pins until their owner changes
them; report an unexpected pin change before the affected integration. An unpinned
moving target is ordinary integration within the shared agreement. The delivery
owner judges where changed interactions need renewed review. A new commit hash
alone does not invalidate applicable evidence. Advance only authorized destinations
with applicable review and user acceptance. Report revision and actual delivery state.

Codex exposes /review-branch and /review-commit for engineering review.
