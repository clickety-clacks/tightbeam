# Subtraction

Question mechanisms and process that do not serve the agreed outcome. Consider
removing an unnecessary requirement or accepting a clearly described limitation
when that serves the product better than adding machinery. Bring changes to product
intent, scope or acceptance to the PO through the responsible owner. Continue
separable authorized work. Use repeated review churn as evidence to reconsider the
approach; no fixed round count decides that judgment. Record material decisions and
their reasons in the ordinary work record. Preserve explicit constraints and required
verification.
