# Agentic-engineering intake

Use existing organization instructions and supported defaults. Resolve missing
information when it affects the next authorized work; do not turn intake into a
mandatory questionnaire or a hold on separable work.

Establish the product outcome, constraints, addressed delivery owner and PO, and the
product spirit reference. Reuse an adequate existing arrangement. Team-design helps
when the arrangement itself needs deliberate reasoning.

Determine permitted model families, hosts and runnable harnesses. Use the shared
working set and engineering activity orders as the starting policy, adapting them
within authority to actual availability. Selection mechanics live in
`guidance/preferred-models.md`; engineering rows live in
`kungfu/agentic-engineering/preferred-models.md`.

Read the repository's verification requirements and the organization's completion
rules. Preserve truthful evidence, independent review and explicit user acceptance
where required. Independent reviewers need separate authorship and adequate
capability; mixed-mode family preference does not make single-family operation
ineligible.

Record material product or capability decisions in their existing homes. A new
bundle election is not authority to change another owner's product commitments,
reparent active sessions or discard unfinished work.
