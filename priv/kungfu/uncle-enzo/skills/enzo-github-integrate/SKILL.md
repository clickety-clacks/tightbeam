---
name: enzo-github-integrate
description: Apply a repository’s elected review and integration procedure.
---

Read the base repository rules and any applicable CODEOWNERS decisions on the exact PR. Recheck the head and required evidence before acting. Follow the repository's selection of manual merge or queue, independent judgment, and squash, merge or rebase. Record the resulting OID and verify it is reachable from the authorized base. Keep the proposed head, queue test commit and integration commit distinguishable.

An organization can copy an elective rule from the accompanying template into its own identity rules and choose its selectors. The evidence template applies to explicitly targeted maintainer assignments. Adapt its scope to the local workflow. Independent-review policy may separately compare the exact canonical revision in a completion request to the latest linked reviewer judgment. Repository protections still apply outside Tightbeam.
