# Standing directives — the base vocabulary

A standing directive is how a principal instructs its desk: durable, ordered,
queryable — filed as a directive-kind attest on the delegation card, never spoken in
chat. The desk is told, not smart; directives are the telling. The contract:

- A directive is `key: value` prose with a designated key from the vocabulary below.
- **Latest wins, per key.** Each key's newest filing is the policy; older filings
  remain on the record as history, not as law.
- **No designated key, no policy.** A filing without a designated key is advice —
  the desk may weigh it, but no gate may cite it.
- **Cancellation is a filing.** To cancel a key, file it again with value `off`.
  Nothing is deleted; the record shows the policy and its end.

The five base keys (seed anatomy — a principal uses them as they are; extending the
vocabulary or reshaping these five is a skeletal change, lawful through the identity
seam, never silent):

- `focus:` — what the principal is on right now. The desk weighs every delivery
  against it; it is the standing answer to "does this change what my principal is
  doing?"
- `interrupt-only-for:` — the traffic that may spend the principal's turn ahead of a
  turn boundary. Everything else waits for the digest, inside its class ceiling.
- `digest:` — how batched traffic should land: grouping, emphasis, cadence within
  the ceilings. Shapes the brief, never the record.
- `dnd-until:` — no summons before the stated time. A time, always — do-not-disturb
  with no end is a state whose exit is someone else's decision, and no such state
  exists here.
- `escalate-to:` — the named role or session for a genuine exception the desk cannot
  answer and must not sit on. It is not a route for ordinary lifecycle updates,
  unanswered peer questions, or a routine appeal to Main. Unset, a genuine exception
  follows the principal's recorded chain.

**Personal never overrides skeletal.** A directive shapes only its own principal's
attention — that is why the principal may file one instantly, no permission asked:
the mind that overrides the reflex is the mind whose hand is in the fire. It never
lowers a floor the org set: the starvation watermark escalates a quiet obligation or
unanswered decision past its floor regardless of any `dnd-until`, and `algedonic`
traffic bypasses every desk and every directive, always. Nobody silences someone
else's alarm — shared vocabulary, floors, and org-wide routing change only on the
org's law path.
