# Staffing the organs — triggers, never spawns

The substrate never staffs. Organs arrive when load demands and the org decides —
one decision each, made by a mind on evidence, executed as a cited spawn by that
mind's DESK (minds decide, desks execute; a principal still deskless performs its
own decision, citation on the filing all the same). What ships is the
inevitability, never the employee: the archetypes sit in the seed with their
charters ready and their books empty, and this page names the signals that say it
is time.

Day one, a fresh org has exactly one guardian arrangement:

- **The prodder is always on**, escalating to Main. It is the deterministic
  starvation floor — any obligation or unanswered decision quiet past its watermark
  escalates, judged by nobody. Its rings land on Main because **Main is the day-one
  nurse**: until the org hires better, the root session adjudicates its own rings.

Two hires wait in the seed, unstaffed:

- **Hire Avasarala when the prodder's ring-rate warrants it** — when adjudicating
  rings is displacing Main's judgment work. Read it from rows: the ring wakes Main
  received, `coordination-share` on Main's session. There is no shipped threshold;
  the number at which triage deserves its own mind is your org's first casebook
  judgment, and shipping it would be shipping our case law.
- **Hire Miller when ledger depth or rumination cadence warrants it** — when the
  org's law, charters, and casebook have grown past what anyone audits in passing,
  or when "are we still doing what we said" has become a recurring rumination
  instead of an occasional glance. Same rule: the trigger is named, the threshold
  is yours.

No auto-spawn, ever. A trigger in this document is information for a mind, not a
rule for the substrate — the day a threshold spawns an employee is the day the
substrate started staffing, and it never does. One office each: the mind records
the decision, its desk performs the spawn carrying the citation, and the organ is
on shift.
